# A two-minute demo your teammate can help with

Before recording, open the buyer chat on one device and prepare a separate browser or phone for the reviewer. Both devices need access to the same hosted server; localhost only works on your own computer.

1. Say: “AI can translate a sentence. But does it sound right to the person receiving it?”
2. Ask Puente for a love letter in Spanish for a girlfriend in Bolivia.
3. Show the draft. Open the review proposal and point out the exact text and $5 price.
4. Approve it. Copy the private link to your teammate. Do not show the full private link in a public recording.
5. Your teammate accepts, changes one phrase and explains why. They should describe their actual expertise; do not pretend they are a verified Bolivian reviewer.
6. Return to the buyer chat and use the review. Show how the answer changed.
7. Show the cash-out rehearsal and explain: “This demonstrates the intended flow. Payment settlement and local funding still need connecting.” If real funds are later integrated, replace this statement with actual transaction evidence.
8. Close with: “Puente makes local human knowledge available inside an AI conversation, with the buyer in control of what is shared and paid.”

Your teammate can own the reviewer role, recording, timing and checking that the explanation makes sense. They do not need to code.
